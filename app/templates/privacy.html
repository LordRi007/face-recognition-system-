Biometric Privacy, Informed Consent & Security Policy
Version 1.0 (GDPR & CCPA Aligned)
1. Purpose and Voluntary Participation

The Face Recognition Management System (FRMS) is developed strictly for authenticated identity verification and voluntary attendance management within authorized organizations. Biometric enrollment is strictly voluntary. Enrolled individuals are provided with clear, affirmative consent options prior to camera activation. Under no circumstances does FRMS perform covert surveillance, mass scanning of crowds, or identification of unknown individuals without their claimed identity.

2. Mathematical Vector Storage & Zero-Raw-Photo Policy

FRMS converts facial landmarks into a non-reversible, 512-dimensional floating-point mathematical vector using the FaceNet512 neural architecture. Raw camera snapshots and uploaded images are never stored in the database or persisted on disk. They are processed in volatile memory and immediately discarded. Stored embedding vectors are encrypted at rest using AES-256 (Fernet) encryption keys stored in isolated server environment variables.

3. Data Retention and Right to Deletion

Biometric embeddings are retained only for the duration of the user's active affiliation with the organization. Users or authorized administrators may revoke consent and permanently purge all facial embeddings at any time via the User Profile or User Management interface. Upon account deletion, all associated biometric templates and vectors are immediately and irreversibly wiped.

4. Alternative Non-Biometric Accommodations

In accordance with international fair labor and privacy standards, no individual is penalized, disciplined, or denied access solely because facial verification fails or because they choose not to participate in biometric scanning. FRMS includes an integrated Alternative PIN Verification method and allows authorized supervisor manual check-in with complete audit trails.

5. Threat Model and Known Limitations

Biometric systems inherently operate on probabilistic pattern matching rather than absolute cryptographic keys. FRMS accounts for the following known technical considerations:

  • False Rejections (FRR): Changes in facial hair, heavy makeup, direct harsh backlight, or camera blur may yield distances above the 0.30 cosine cutoff. The alternative PIN method guarantees uninterrupted check-in.
  • False Acceptances (FAR): A model threshold of 0.30 cosine distance is calibrated to minimize false positive matches during 1-to-1 claimed identity verification.
  • Presentation Attacks (Spoofing): FRMS applies high-frequency texture variance and edge analysis to detect printed paper or flat screen presentations. For high-security environments, supervised kiosk terminals or 3D depth cameras are recommended.
  • Demographic Performance Differences: Pretrained models may exhibit minor variance across diverse lighting and skin tones. FRMS uses dynamic illumination quality gates to prompt users for adequate frontal light prior to capture.
Security Contact: For inquiries regarding biometric data storage or to request an automated data export under GDPR/CCPA data subject rights, please contact the designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) at your organization.
